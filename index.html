01 / COMING SOON

A new chapter for Nebula.

Nebula lets you play Super Mario Galaxy natively, using your own copy of the game. v2 adds ray-traced lighting, high-refresh motion, more controller options, mods and achievements.

The original adventure stays at the centre: the same levels, music and movement, with settings that fit your screen and the way you play.

Graphics

Ray-traced lighting →

Sunlight, shadows, reflections and water, with lamps, fire and Lumas lighting the world around them.

Display

Play at your refresh rate →

Extra frames follow the game’s own motion, bringing smoother movement to 75, 120, 144, 165, 240 Hz and other displays.

Graphics

A wider view →

Internal resolution up to 16× and aspect ratios from 4:3 to 32:9, with sharp text and a HUD that fits your screen.

Controls

Your choice of controller →

Keyboard and mouse, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch controllers, Joy-Con, Steam controllers and a real Wii Remote with Nunchuk.

In game

Less waiting, more playing →

Skip cutscenes, speed up dialogue and change settings from an in-game menu. Import Miis for your save-file icons.

Mods

Mods and texture packs →

Import Riivolution mods, file replacements, Dolphin-format texture packs and HD movies from the Mods menu or by drag and drop.

Progress

34 achievements →

Track your progress with achievement pop-ups and a Luma’s board in the Comet Observatory, with goals and lifetime play stats.

Saves

Bring your save along →

Import a Dolphin save, back up your progress and move saves through the launcher. Your current saves are backed up before an import.

02 / START PLAYING

Bring your own game.

  1. 01

    Check your copy

    For v2, you need Super Mario Galaxy for Wii, North America (RMGE01), revision 0. Prepare an ISO, RVZ or extracted game folder.

  2. 02

    Check your PC

    Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit), an AVX2-capable CPU, a Direct3D 12 GPU, 8 GB RAM and about 5 GB of space.

  3. 03

    Install with Nebula Setup

    Choose your game, install, then open Nebula from the Start menu. The setup guide covers the steps and your first settings.

04 / QUICK ANSWERS

Before you launch.

Is Nebula free?

Yes. Nebula is free software under the GNU GPL v3. You need your own copy of Super Mario Galaxy; game files are not included.

Is v2 available yet?

Not yet. v2 is coming soon. The current public release is v1.0.1. The v2 features described here are for the upcoming update.

Is this an emulator?

No. In v2, Super Mario Galaxy runs as native code built from SMGCommunity’s Petari decompilation. The original levels, music, story and gameplay come from your own game.

Which copy of the game do I need?

The supported copy is the North American Wii release, RMGE01, revision 0. For v2, prepare an ISO, RVZ or extracted folder. Support for other regions has not been announced.

Can I play on Linux, Steam Deck, Android or Mac?

Windows is available now, and an Android beta is released. Linux and SteamOS are in development; macOS is planned.

Can I use a Dolphin save?

Yes. Close the game, then use Advanced → Import saves in the launcher. See the save guide for the steps.

Will all Wii mods work?

No. File replacements and compatible texture packs work, but mods that patch the Wii’s machine code may not run in a native game. Check the mod guide before importing.

Is Nebula made by Nintendo?

No. Nebula is an independent project by the Nebula developers. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Nintendo.
