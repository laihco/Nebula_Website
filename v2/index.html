01 / THE NEXT UPDATE

The game you know, running natively.

Nebula v2 runs Super Mario Galaxy as native code built from SMGCommunity’s Petari decompilation. You play the original levels, story and music from your own copy, with new options for how the game looks and feels.

v2 is coming soon. There is no announced release date yet. The current public download is v1.0.1; the features below describe the upcoming update.

02 / WHAT’S NEW

Choose how you play.

Graphics

Ray-traced lighting →

Sunlight, shadows, reflections and water, with lamps, fire and Lumas lighting the world around them.

Display

Play at your refresh rate →

Extra frames follow the game’s own motion, bringing smoother movement to 75, 120, 144, 165, 240 Hz and other displays.

Graphics

A wider view →

Internal resolution up to 16× and aspect ratios from 4:3 to 32:9, with sharp text and a HUD that fits your screen.

Controls

Your choice of controller →

Keyboard and mouse, Xbox, PlayStation, Switch controllers, Joy-Con, Steam controllers and a real Wii Remote with Nunchuk.

In game

Less waiting, more playing →

Skip cutscenes, speed up dialogue and change settings from an in-game menu. Import Miis for your save-file icons.

Mods

Mods and texture packs →

Import Riivolution mods, file replacements, Dolphin-format texture packs and HD movies from the Mods menu or by drag and drop.

Progress

34 achievements →

Track your progress with achievement pop-ups and a Luma’s board in the Comet Observatory, with goals and lifetime play stats.

Saves

Bring your save along →

Import a Dolphin save, back up your progress and move saves through the launcher. Your current saves are backed up before an import.

03 / A CLOSER LOOK

Light, water and motion.

04 / IN GAME

Useful changes, close at hand.

  • Open settings from the pause menu or with F1. Graphics, controls, audio and mods are available without leaving the game.
  • Press + to skip supported cutscenes, tutorials and boss introductions. A confirmation appears before the skip.
  • Choose original, 2×, 3× or Instant dialogue speed, including the storybook.
  • Set separate music, effects, voices, ambience, cutscene and Wii Remote speaker volumes in Audio.
  • Import Miis for save-file icons and show your current galaxy and mission in Discord.
  • Export saves, import a Dolphin save, or restore a backup from the launcher.
  • Track 34 achievements and play statistics through a Luma’s board in the Observatory.
